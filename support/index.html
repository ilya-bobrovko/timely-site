Support

Timely — tasks and habits for iPhone.

Something broken, something confusing, or something you wish the app did? Write to timely.app.help@gmail.com. One person reads that mailbox and answers every message, usually within a few days.

Reporting a bug

Three lines make a report far easier to act on:

  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Timely version, shown at the bottom of the Profile screen.

A screenshot or a screen recording helps more than any description.

Restoring a purchase

Open the Profile screen and tap Restore purchases. The purchase is tied to the Apple Account that paid for it, so make sure that is the account signed in on the device. Billing, refunds and subscription cancellation are handled by Apple, not by us — use Apple's billing support for those.

Where your data lives

Your tasks are kept on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud storage when iCloud sync is on. We hold no copy and cannot restore data that was deleted on your device. The privacy policy describes this in full.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings, then Timely, then Notifications. If they are allowed and reminders still do not arrive, write to us with the time you expected one and we will look into it.
