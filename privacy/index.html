Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Timely is a task and habit tracker for iPhone, made by Ilya Babrouka, an independent developer. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, who can read it and how long it is kept. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Your tasks stay on your device

Everything you create in Timely — tasks, habits, tags, completion history, reminder times and your settings — is stored in a database on your iPhone. None of it is sent to a server of ours. We have no copy of it and cannot read it.

iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is on, that same data is also kept in the private database of your own iCloud account, so your devices stay in step. Apple holds it under your Apple Account, in a container named iCloud.com.bobrovko.timely. Only you and Apple can reach it; we cannot. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Turning iCloud off for Timely in iOS Settings stops the sync.

One identifier, used by two services

When you first open Timely, the app generates a random identifier for the installation and keeps it in the app's own preferences on your device. It is not your Apple Account, not a device serial number, and not an advertising identifier. It is removed when you delete the app.

Both services below receive that same identifier, which means your usage statistics and your purchase records are tied to each other by it.

Usage statistics

Timely uses Mixpanel to count how the app is used, so we can tell which features matter and where people get stuck.

What is sent is the name of a screen you opened and the fact that something happened: a task was created, completed, skipped or deleted, a tag was made, reminders were switched on or off, an onboarding step was shown, the paywall was shown, a purchase was started, finished, cancelled or failed. Each of these carries a few labels describing the shape of the thing, never its content — for example whether a task repeats and how often (daily, weekly, monthly), and whether it has a time, a reminder, tags or a deadline.

What is never sent:

  • the title or text of any task, habit, tag or note;
  • the dates or times you chose;
  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • your location, your contacts, your photos, your calendars.

Mixpanel's own automatic event collection is turned off, and the mobile carrier, radio type and Wi-Fi fields are excluded before anything is sent.

Purchases

Timely offers a paid plan. The payment itself is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to tell whether your purchase is active and to restore it on your other devices. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt issued by Apple and the identifier described above.

Crash reports

We read crash reports through Xcode Organizer, Apple's own tool. Those reports reach us only if you allowed sharing them with app developers in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements. Apple strips them of anything identifying you before we see them, and we add no crash reporting of our own.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your own device. Nothing about them leaves your iPhone, and the app does not send push notifications from a server.

How long it is kept

  • Mixpanel events — two years. Mixpanel deletes events automatically after two years, on an ongoing basis.
  • RevenueCat's purchase record — until it is deleted.
  • Everything on your device, including the identifier — until you delete it or delete the app.

No advertising, no selling

Timely shows no ads and contains no advertising networks. It does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your data and do not share it with data brokers.

Children

Timely is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Delete a task in the app and it is gone from the device, and from your iCloud copy on the next sync. Delete the app and the local database goes with it, and so does the identifier — the app holds nothing that survives its own removal.

Two things are not removed by deleting the app, and we state this as a fact rather than a promise about them: the statistics already sent to Mixpanel, which that service deletes on its own two-year schedule, and RevenueCat's record of your purchase, which is kept until it is deleted.

Write to us about any of this and we will answer. That is the whole of what we promise here: a reply to a written request. We make no further commitment about locating or erasing particular records.
